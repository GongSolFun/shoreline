The estate

Lagunas

Lagunas is the canal neighborhood inside Ciudad Mayakoba, on the federal highway at km 298. Apartment towers and lower villas sit around the lagoons. On the grounds: three private pools, one communal pool, a gym, and office space. Private security at the Lagunas entrance, and Mayakoba has its own gate that checks a security ID before you enter. The beach is about ten minutes away; Cancún airport about half an hour.
